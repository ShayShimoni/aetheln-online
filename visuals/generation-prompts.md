# Generation Prompt Set

> **Superseded Kell notes.** The Kell prompts in this file (living skin,
> mineral crown, mantle) are the superseded design, kept as history. Current
> canon: `docs/playable-peoples.md` (Kell section) and the
> `docs/art-style-guide.md`.

All images were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. These
are the reusable final prompt briefs; canonical details come from the Aetheln
design documents.

## 01 - Main Menu

Create a practical 16:9 PC title screen for a stylized dark-fantasy action
MMORPG. Show Glasswake Reach at dusk, with grassland and ruins fused into
rippling black glass beneath the wounded Waking Star. Use etched dark metal,
smoky glass, repaired seams, restrained Ember orange, and spectral cyan. Render
the exact title `AETHELN ONLINE` and the menu items `CONTINUE`, `PLAY`,
`SETTINGS`, `ACCESSIBILITY`, `CREDITS`, and `QUIT`. Keep `PLAY` focused. Do not
use faction heraldry, generic fantasy crests, skulls, or unreadable black-on-
black controls.

## 02 - Playable Peoples

Create a clean 16:9 full-body lineup of male and female Aurin, Kell, and Vesh
at equivalent visual scale. Aurin are fully human and wear repaired layered
workwear. Kell have mostly living clay/ash/slate skin with mineral crown,
selected facial planes, mantle, upper-torso structures, and joint caps; they
are not stone golems. Vesh have matte dusk skin, two reflective eyes, a flush
mouth seam, conventional hair, restrained inner light, and paired living
back-veils. Use grounded painterly stylization, neutral studio lighting, and
the exact category labels `AURIN`, `KELL`, and `VESH`. Do not imply faction,
class, hitbox, reach, or stat differences.

## 03 - Aurin Bulwark Equipment

Create a 16:9 production concept sheet showing one coherent Aurin Bulwark armor
identity fitted to male and female bodies, with separate readable views of a
large pavise and spear. Use repaired maintained steel, dark wood, heavy cloth,
dark leather, reinforced seams, restrained rust cloth, and small Ember accents.
The shield should resemble a portable gate. Keep both variants practical,
animation-friendly, and equivalent in function. Avoid final heraldry,
impossible spikes, oversized pauldrons, sexualized armor, and giant weapons.

## 04 - Branmark

Create a 16:9 arrival view of Branmark, the Tithe City, as a major Dawn-
aligned Aurin fortress settlement without declaring it the final faction
capital. Show concentric inhabited defenses, guarded tithe gates, signal
towers, barracks, patched masonry, surviving homes and workshops, and a severe
central Ember registry above a failing light-well. Use cold dawn, fog, and
tightly rationed warm lamps. The city is disciplined, burdened, inhabited, and
defensible rather than evil. Do not use final faction symbols or imply racial
ownership.

## 05 - Glasswake Reach

Create a 16:9 third-person-height environment concept of a traversable
grassland plain fused into rippling black and smoky glass by the Duskbreak.
Show buried ruins, a distant cracked spire, navigable paths and elevation, and
a small living pocket growing around a newly fallen Ember. Use cold slate and
faded earth with restrained warm life and faint cyan glass reflections. The
glass must read as catastrophic landscape material, not water or ice. Do not
assign a final faction, ownership, or PvP state.

## 06 - Character Selection

Create a practical 16:9 CommonUI-feasible character-selection screen for the
pre-faction cooperative slice. Show an Aurin, Kell, and Vesh portrait roster;
select an adult female Kell whose living skin covers most of her body and whose
mineral anatomy is limited to crown, facial planes, mantle, upper-torso
accents, and joints. Use a safe-hub overlook, clear controller focus, smoky
glass panels, and restrained Ember accents. Render exactly `SELECT CHARACTER`,
`KELL`, `LEVEL 1`, `FACTION UNASSIGNED`, `ENTER WORLD`, `CREATE CHARACTER`,
`DELETE`, and `BACK`; focus `ENTER WORLD`. Do not show Doctrine, power scores,
racial bonuses, or faction heraldry.

## 07 - UI Style System

Create a 16:9 reusable PC game UI component board suitable for Unreal
CommonUI. Include title treatment, normal/hover/focused/pressed/disabled
buttons, tabs, modal, settings rows, roster card, tooltip, loading indicator,
keyboard and generic gamepad prompts, focus ring, dividers, and color/material
swatches. Use charcoal, cold slate, iron, smoke glass, parchment, Ember orange,
and spectral cyan. Components should be crisp, readable, accessible without
color alone, and friendly to reusable nine-slice construction. Avoid ornate
gothic clutter, gold-heavy luxury, mobile-game styling, and sci-fi HUD motifs.
