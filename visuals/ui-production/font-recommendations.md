# Typography

## Display: Cinzel

Use Cinzel for the product title, screen titles, and rare high-level headings.
Do not use it for paragraphs, settings rows, tooltips, or dense information.

Cinzel is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license permits
use, embedding, modification, and redistribution subject to its conditions:

- Font files: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cinzel
- License: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/cinzel/OFL.txt

Recommended weights:

- Aetheln Online title: 500 or 600
- Screen titles: 500

## Interface: Source Sans 3

Use Source Sans 3 for controls, settings, descriptions, tooltips, numbers, and
accessibility-critical text. It was designed for interface environments and is
distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Font repository: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans
- License: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans/blob/main/LICENSE.md

Recommended weights:

- Controls: 600
- Body: 400
- Secondary labels: 400

## Shipping Note

Download font files only from the linked official repositories. Preserve their
license and copyright files beside the third-party font sources. Verify glyph
coverage for every planned localization before locking the family.
