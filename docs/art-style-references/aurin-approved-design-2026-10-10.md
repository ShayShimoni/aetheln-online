## Design and use

- Male: mature Human face with simplified sculpted planes, dark curls with gray,
  short beard, sturdy muscular athletic build, charcoal shorts and bare feet.
- Female: athletic feminine physique, fuller chest, defined waist and hips,
  softer feminine face, dark neck-length carre bob, charcoal short-sleeved crop
  top, knee-length pants and bare feet.
- Both: complete frontal A-pose, open hands, natural Human anatomy, warm gray
  studio background, polished stylized 3D volumes, restrained facial detail,
  distinct matte skin/cloth shading and soft grounded shadows.

Start from this pair for derived views and fitted clothing/armor. The individual
faces, hairstyles, builds and modeling clothes are selected examples, not a
single mandatory Human preset or a faction/heritage uniform. Preserve the full
canonical Human appearance range. No race, sex or appearance choice changes
