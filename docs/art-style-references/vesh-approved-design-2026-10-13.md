## Design to preserve

- Athletic humanoid creature proportions, a modest forward shoulder set, and
  a visible neck. The male has a broader chest and shoulders; the female has
  a slimmer athletic silhouette and gentler face contour.
- Close black coat with broad white sculpted fur fields at the throat,
  chest, shoulders, outer arms, forearms, flanks, outer thighs, and calves.
  A dark central V separates the white chest fields; the abdomen remains dark.
- Rigid ivory-gray natural face, dark lateral cheeks, broad geometric planes,
  tapered chin, and narrow golden luminous eyes. The face is anatomy, not a
  removable mask over a human face.
- Neutral, restrained dark creature mouth with softly rounded ends. Preserve
  the facial planes; no visible teeth, human lips, nose, or projecting muzzle.
- Two elongated clawed fingers and one opposable thumb per hand; digitigrade
  legs with raised hocks and three-toed clawed paws.
- Compact white sculpted mane: short and spiky on the male; swept back with
  a longer nape on the selected female. Keep the neck visible.
- Polished stylized 3D volumes, controlled satin highlights, simplified fur
  clumps, clear shadows, and a neutral studio presentation. Fur is not armor,
  feathers, or scales; incidental surface noise is not a required feature.

The pair are unarmored anatomy references with opaque coat coverage. Helmets,
gauntlets, and boots must fit their biological features without removing them.
Appearance does not change the shared combat capsule, reach, contact timing,
weapon sockets, stats, class access, or faction access.

## Validation boundary

Approval establishes the visual design, not production readiness. These PNGs
contain no meshes or rigs. Turnarounds, topology, jaw deformation, digitigrade
IK, grip alignment, equipment fitting, budgets, and in-engine tests remain
unverified. Follow the [art validation gate](../playable-peoples.md#art-validation-gate)
before production acceptance. This record does not claim a commit, PR, merge,
or delivery-board transition.
