# Playable Peoples of Aetheln

## Document Status

This is the canonical anatomy, visual identity, cultural-heritage, character
creation, animation, and equipment-presentation reference for the playable
peoples of Aetheln.

The Game Design Bible governs high-level product behavior. This document
governs playable-people detail. Characters, settlements, classes, factions,
progression, and technical architecture remain governed by their specialized
canonical documents.

## Shared Canon

The three confirmed playable peoples are Aurin, Kell, and Vesh.

- The Waking Star dreamed each people separately into existence.
- All three peoples predate the Duskbreak. No confirmed account identifies
  which people was dreamed first.
- Aurin, Kell, and Vesh are distinct biologies and do not produce biological
  hybrids with one another. Cross-people families form through partnership,
  adoption, and chosen kinship.
- Each people maintains heritage languages alongside a shared common language.
- Every playable people supports male and female adult characters across a
  broad visible age range.
- Every people and sex may use every supported class and join either player
  faction.
- Race, sex, body, age, face, voice, and other appearance choices never change
  attributes, combat power, collision, reach, traces, timing, readable
  telegraphs, loot probability, or progression.
- All people use a narrow, overlapping visual-height range and broad curated
  body presets, including lean, soft, muscular, and heavy-set directions.
- Male and female presentations must read as clearly distinct within each
  people while retaining broad overlap in height, build, age presentation, and
  voice. Sex-linked face, body, and secondary-anatomy anchors are defined below
  and remain inside the shared combat envelope.

Visual designs must not reduce the peoples to renamed stock fantasy races,
animal heads on humanoid bodies, or extreme playable size classes.

## Culture, Faction, and Individual Identity

Each people has recognizable heritage materials, motifs, naming traditions,
and movement language. Those roots do not prescribe personality, profession,
class, homeland, or faction.

Each people's heritage identity carries a stable semantic ID with a working
display name (Issue #104): Aurin use `heritage.menders_brace` (Mender's Brace),
Kell use `heritage.memory_nail` (Memory Nail), and Vesh use
`heritage.foreseen_path` (Guided Path). The Vesh working display name replaces
Foreseen Path; the stable ID is unchanged. IDs remain stable when display names
change. Heritage is narrative and presentation identity only; it never changes
combat statistics, hitboxes, reach, timing, or loot.

- Aurin are not biologically aligned with the Crowned Ledger (the provisional
  faction display name superseding Dawn Concordat under Issue #104).
- Kell are not biologically aligned with the Hundred Witnesses (superseding
  Unbound Flame under Issue #104).
- Vesh are not biologically neutral.
- Any people may live within, inherit, learn, or wear another people's culture.
- Cultural equipment and cosmetics must have fitted visual variants for every
  supported people and sex.
- Faction identity must come from explicit faction heraldry, clothing,
  objectives, territory, Doctrine, and story presentation rather than anatomy.

## Aurin

### Approved Design Record

On 2026-10-10 the user selected the
[male Aurin v06](art-style-references/aurin-male-approved-2026-10-10.png) and
[female Aurin v13](art-style-references/aurin-female-approved-2026-10-10.png)
as the updated race visual baseline. The male has a sturdy muscular athletic
build, dark curls with gray, a short beard, and simplified mature facial
planes. The female has an athletic feminine build with a fuller chest,
defined waist and hips, a softer feminine face, and a neck-length dark bob.
Both use natural Human anatomy and the polished stylized 3D sculpt finish in
the [Art Style and Generation Guide](art-style-guide.md).

These are full-body frontal A-pose anatomy references, wearing plain charcoal
modeling clothing rather than armor: shorts for the male; a short-sleeved crop
top and knee-length pants for the female. Their clothing, individual faces,
hairstyles, and builds are reference choices, not limits on Aurin customization
or replacements for the heritage clothing language below. The broader Human
appearance range and shared combat rules remain in force.

The [approval and provenance record](art-style-references/aurin-approved-design-2026-10-10.md)
preserves the selected versions, exact prompts, and image identities. Earlier
Aurin studies are historical references. Image approval does not establish a
mesh, rig, equipment fit, or runtime acceptance.

### Identity and Anatomy

Aurin are the human people of Aetheln. "Human" is an out-of-world explanatory
term; people in Aetheln call them Aurin.

Aurin use fully human anatomy and the full natural range of human skin tones,
hair colors, hair textures, faces, builds, and visible aging. They have no
innate glow, glass, Ember mark, mutation, or supernatural organ. Individual
magic exposure, injury, scars, cosmetics, and Duskbreak effects remain
individual conditions rather than Aurin biology.

Male and female Aurin use clearly distinct base human face, torso-form,
fat-distribution, and musculature directions across overlapping heights and
limb lengths. Every sex still supports the full curated body-preset,
age-presentation, and voice ranges.

### Visual Language

Aurin should feel resilient and burdened rather than pristine or generically
heroic.

- **Materials:** maintained steel, dark wood, heavy woven cloth, visible
  mending, and inherited pieces.
- **Motifs:** repaired lines, joined breaks, reinforced seams, layered borders,
  and tally marks used for family records, vows, debts, or survival counts.
- **Heritage palette:** charcoal, iron, rust-red Ember warmth, and restrained
  gold light.
- **Clothing silhouette:** layered workwear with structured shoulders, wrapped
  waists, overlapping panels, and visible repairs.
- **Markings:** the full range of human scars, tattoos, paint, cosmetic
  pigments, ritual marks, and natural features.

These are heritage defaults, not compulsory clothing or a faction uniform.

### Culture, Names, and Motion

Aurin cultures argue over whether survival means repairing inherited systems or
dismantling them to build something genuinely new.

Aurin heritage names are grounded and familiar. Flowing given names such as
Kaelen, Serra, and Aldrec may pair with terse lineage, place, or trade surnames
such as Dross, Vale, and Fane.

Aurin movement is grounded and purposeful. Weight settles clearly, gestures
conserve effort, and actions appear practiced. This movement identity is
presentation only and does not change gameplay timing.

Aurin voices cover a broad human range. Accent and vocal character may express
region and culture independently from faction or class.

## Kell

### Approved Design Record

On 2026-10-10 the user selected
[male Kell image 57](art-style-references/57-quartz-balanced-hands-feet.png) and
[female Kell image 59](art-style-references/59-quartz-amethyst-female-refined.png)
as the new Kell. These are the canonical visual baseline for the race. Use the
[Art Style and Generation Guide](art-style-guide.md) for their polished 3D
rendering finish.

This revision replaces the former living-skin body with its localized mineral
frame, crown, and shoulder mantle. It is a revision of the race's design and
history, not a transformation that every Kell underwent during the Duskbreak.
Earlier studies, prompts, and reviews retain their historical meaning but no
longer define Kell anatomy. The selected front concepts approve the design
direction; the production validation gate below still applies.

### Identity and Anatomy

Kell are living crystalline people. Pale ivory quartz grows in overlapping,
curved anatomical plates over a deeper network of smoky amethyst crystal.
Both layers are living parts of the body. The darker interior connects the
neck, torso, limbs, and joints, allowing the layered form to articulate. Kell
are born, grow, age, and die; their mineral appearance does not make them
manufactured constructs or animated deposits of ore.

Required Kell anatomy includes:

- an expressive face with sculpted brow, cheek, and jaw planes, a broad low
  flat nose, and a defined mouth;
- glowing violet eyes with readable eye contours;
- long external pointed ears with dark crystal accents;
- paired swept quartz horns rooted above the temples, curving outward and
  back, with dark amethyst insets;
- amethyst accents at the forehead, temples, cheeks, and inner ears;
- flowing quartz plates across the torso and limbs, with darker crystal bands
  visible between them;
- articulated clawed hands with an opposable thumb and usable weapon grips;
  and
- grounded feet with a heel and three articulated forward toes ending in
  quartz claws.

Kell are hairless. Their head remains expressive and compact enough for fitted
helmets; it has neither an animal muzzle nor a separate oversized crown. The
neck is substantial but allows a readable collar and shoulder line. Hands and
feet stay balanced with the body. Horns, ears, plate contours, and claws remain
inside the shared presentation bounds and do not expand combat collision.

The approved baseline uses pale ivory outer quartz and dark smoky violet
amethyst beneath it. Sparse mineral veins and controlled facets enrich broad,
smooth forms. The interior has restrained local radiance, and the eyes glow
more clearly. These are ordinary Kell anatomy, not Ember attunement, a power
resource, or a combat-state signal. Alternate mineral families and glow hues
are not established by the earlier exploration; their availability remains TBD.

Living plates are biological surfaces, not equipped armor. Horns, claws, and
crystal light grant no racial damage, defense, detection, healing, or other
gameplay advantage. Exact hand digit topology and grip deformation remain
production TBDs to resolve from the selected concepts and shared weapon-grip
tests, rather than carrying forward the former five-digit requirement.

### Sex, Age, and Variation

The selected male has a broader chest, shoulders, and jaw with moderately
full musculature. The selected female has less muscle bulk, a softer brow and
jaw, and an athletic feminine torso and waist-to-hip transition. Both share
the same horn family, external ears, layered construction, glowing eyes, and
clawed extremities. Sex differences do not require separate incompatible horn
or plate systems. Each sex retains broad body-preset, age, and voice ranges
within the same visual scale, limb reach, and combat envelope.

Face presets may vary the brow, cheeks, jaw, mouth, and low nasal planes while
retaining the Kell anchors. Horn form, plate pattern, surface tones, finish,
and wear provide variation without replacing the living layered structure.

Kell are very long-lived and may span several Aurin generations, but they are
not immortal. Exact lifespan years remain TBD. Growth and ordinary healing
renew living crystal; older surfaces carry inclusions, softened edges, fine
chips, and repair seams. Aging does not continually enlarge the body, horns,
or combat silhouette. Mineral renewal is a description of life over time,
not an accelerated combat-regeneration ability.

Underlying anatomy remains balanced, with natural asymmetry from life and
repair. Voluntary surface engraving and inlay may record personal history;
they are cosmetic practices, not compulsory marks or sources of power.

### Visual Language

Kell should feel like living geology shaped into a graceful, powerful body.
Sweeping plate curves and dark crystalline connections distinguish their
silhouette while keeping face, gestures, and emotion readable.

- **Materials:** satin quartz, deep amethyst, maintained dark metal, woven
  cloth, crafted memory glass, and selective inlay.
- **Motifs:** overlapping growth lines, joined fractures, and a visible seam
  allowed to remain after repair.
- **Heritage palette:** ivory, smoky violet, charcoal, deep umber, and restrained
  warm-metal accents. Biological color does not indicate faction.
- **Clothing silhouette:** fitted collars, articulated layers, and clean
  shoulder shapes that follow the body without obscuring its construction.

Armor remains separate wearable equipment, with accommodations for horns,
ears, plates, hands, and feet. Legendary suits may frame the body with stronger
shapes and controlled light while preserving fit and combat readability.

### Origin, Renewal, and Memory

The Waking Star dreamed the Kell as living crystal, separately from the Aurin
and Vesh. The first Kell awakened among the quartz-bearing depths of the canyon
that became Graefell, before the Duskbreak. No account establishes which people
the Star dreamed first. Ordinary Kell life continues through generations;
the race does not depend on fresh catastrophes to create its people.

Kell experience continuity through changing material. A repaired surface can
remain visibly different while the person is still whole. A long life therefore
leaves a succession of joined layers rather than a perfectly preserved original
form. Care, craft, and remembrance are intertwined in their heritage: keeping
something alive requires tending it, and tending it may require accepting
change.

Kell cultures argue over whether every memory must be preserved or whether
forgetting, privacy, and release are necessary for life. Some treat every seam
as a debt to the past. Others deliberately leave a repair undecorated or refuse
to name what caused it. Neither practice describes all Kell or determines a
person's faction.

Their bodies show growth and injury, not readable recordings of thoughts,
ancestry, or other people's lives. Memory preservation remains learned craft
using tools, vessels, and study, open to all peoples. A crafted archive is
distinct from its keeper's living body. The Memory Nail heritage represents
the deliberate act of marking what one chooses to carry forward; it does not
make memories biological property or grant an innate power.

The Duskbreak confronted Kell with crystal's opposite promise: entire places
fixed into inert glass while their living histories were torn apart. Their
continued life did not prove that their archives, families, or knowledge were
safe. Graefell's arguments about ownership became arguments about care: who
may preserve a memory, who must maintain it, and who may consent to its release?

### Names and Motion

Kell heritage names are resonant and weighty. Names such as Corvath and Vothram
favor deep consonants and held internal rhythms without requiring one repeated
suffix.

Kell movement has deliberate weight transfer and fluid articulation between
overlapping forms. Posture and easing convey substance without making them
rigid or slow to respond. Startup, contact, recovery, root motion, and travel
timing remain identical to the shared gameplay source.

Kell voices support broad pitch and character choices with subtle mineral
resonance rather than one fixed vocal archetype.

### Corvath's Exception

Corvath is a unique living-glass Kell created by the Duskbreak. His silhouette
retains the Kell facial, horn, ear, and limb structure, but his whole body is
living catastrophe glass rather than renewing quartz plates and amethyst
connections. His creation and preservation of conscious dying people are
exceptions, not ordinary Kell birth, mineral renewal, or memory craft. His
complete material state is not a heritable subtype or a player appearance.

Vothram forged the instruments used to cause the Duskbreak, confirming that
Kell existed before the catastrophe. Graefell's first Kell awakening therefore
predates the canyon's later glassing.

## Vesh

### Design Record

The 2026-10-10 redesign replaces the former dream-veiled identity with embodied
spirit creatures who follow the Light's guidance. This is a revision of the
people's canonical history, not a recent transformation caused by the Duskbreak.

Use the retained [selected male](art-style-references/vesh-male-selected-2026-10-10.png)
and [current female](art-style-references/vesh-female-current-2026-10-10.png)
for this visual direction. The [results record](art-style-references/character-results-2026-10-10.md)
preserves their exact prompts and separate selection states; final female
visual approval remains pending. Written lore does not silently approve a
production model or every visual option. Earlier Vesh studies and their
back-veil requirements are superseded for new work.

### Origin and Embodied Life

The Waking Star dreamed the Vesh as spirits with lives and physical forms of
their own, separately from Aurin and Kell before the Duskbreak. Their first
communities gathered where the light held a place's living history together.
They learned to attend to the presences carried by its waters, paths, groves,
homes, and remembered lives. No confirmed account establishes which people
the Star dreamed first.

Vesh describe their bodies as light that has learned to remain. That light
holds a tangible, mortal form: it can be touched, injured, clothed, and cared
for. They grow within families, age, and die. Their peoplehood is not created
by possessing another creature or recalling a deceased Aurin or Kell.

Their spiritual kinship is part of their identity, while listening well is a
practice learned through experience, ritual, and consent. An encounter does
not give a listener every history a spirit carries. Other peoples can learn
to meet and interpret these presences; Vesh heritage grants no exclusive
right to speak for them.

The [world cosmology](world-and-settlements.md#spirits-and-the-light) defines
spirits as bounded local continuities, distinct from Wardens, Embers, and
Forgettings. Neither Vesh identity nor spirit contact supplies innate damage,
defense, healing, detection, prophecy, flight, incorporeality, or resurrection.
Ember attunement remains a separate relationship. Nhalen's severance does not
erase his Vesh identity, and ordinary spirit testimony cannot replace the
witnessed record used by the Emberbound's shared restoration rules.

### Identity and Anatomy

The selected design has a fit humanoid silhouette with clearly nonhuman
extremities and a natural geometric face. Its required visual anatomy includes:

- an obsidian-black short velvety coat with clean white markings;
- white throat, paired upper-chest fields divided by a dark central V, outer
  shoulder and arm markings, forearm tufts, flanks, outer thighs, and calves;
  the central abdomen remains dark;
- an ivory-gray faceted face grown as part of the body, with a softened tapered
  lower contour and no visible human face beneath it;
- two narrow golden-yellow luminous eyes, with no visible human eye whites;
- no visible mouth, nose, mouth seam, or required neck vents;
- white hair, with short spiky male and long gathered female hair in the
  retained pair; these examples do not prescribe every hairstyle;
- hands with two elongated fingers and one opposable thumb; and
- solid, softly tapered grounded feet without individual toes or split hooves.

The face is biological identity rather than a removable mask or equipment.
Its planes may vary within the same design family; eye angle, brow, cheeks,
and jaw remain readable without introducing human facial openings. Speech
resonates through the body and face. Eye expression, head inclination, posture,
gesture, and a broad range of subtly harmonic voices carry emotion.

White markings are fur pigment, not glowing veins or separate luminous organs.
Keep emission localized to the golden eyes. Fur remains short and sculpturally
simple, with restrained longer tufts at the existing forearm and calf accents.
There are no biological wings, back-veils, horns, tails, muzzles, or animal ears.
The [art guide](art-style-guide.md) governs the polished 3D finish.

### Sex, Age, and Variation

The retained male uses a lean athletic masculine build, broader shoulders and
chest, and a stronger facial contour. The current female uses a slim athletic
feminine build, gentler waist-to-hip transitions, a softer jaw and cheek contour,
and balanced smaller-looking hands and feet. Both remain inside the shared
visual scale, limb-reach bounds, combat envelope, and broad body-preset range.

Both sexes retain the same face material, golden eyes, short coat, white
marking language, and hand/foot topology. Hair, face, body, voice, and marking
presets provide individual variation; exact supported choices remain TBD.

Vesh live moderately longer than Aurin without approaching Kell longevity.
Exact years remain TBD. Age appears through changes in hair and coat texture,
subtle wear on the natural face, posture, and voice. Their inner coherence can
be wounded, but aging does not make their bodies transparent or change their
combat surface.

### Following the Light

Vesh heritage calls its practice the Guided Path, retaining the stable ID
`heritage.foreseen_path`. A guide asks a listener to notice a life or place that
needs care; the listener must still choose a response. The Light is understood
as a call toward clarity, compassion, and renewal. It is not a law spoken in a
single voice, and a Vesh cannot claim certainty about the Waking Star's intent.

Enlightenment is an ongoing discipline. A listener first quiets personal wants,
hears a presence without demanding agreement, compares its account with other
witnesses, and accepts responsibility for acting under uncertainty. A spirit
may remain silent, depart, remember wrongly, or ask for release rather than
preservation. Listening creates a duty of care rather than ownership.

Families and traveling circles keep shared lantern vigils: they gather living
witnesses and willing spirits, remember specific names, and leave room for
accounts that do not agree. Apprentices accompany experienced keepers on
pilgrimages before conducting their own. These practices are open to other
peoples and are not a substitute for Order training or faction choice.

Their cultural dispute is whether guidance imposes an obligation that must be
followed or a question that must be weighed against other duties. The first
position can become obedience to a charismatic interpreter; the second can
become an excuse never to act. Individuals can be generous, vain, uncertain,
non-observant, or coercive. Spiritual identity does not guarantee moral purity.

Vesh in the Crowned Ledger argue that accountable records and common law
protect fragile witnesses. Vesh in the Hundred Witnesses argue that a community
and its spirits must retain the right to speak or leave. Each can follow the
Light while disputing the other's method. Neither faction owns its guidance.

### Visual Language, Names, and Motion

- **Materials:** dark woven cloth, pale carved wood, satin ivory fittings,
  translucent lantern resin, and restrained golden luminous thread.
- **Motifs:** branching paths that meet without merging, open lantern frames,
  and nested arcs that leave space around a central light.
- **Heritage palette:** obsidian, white, ivory, and small lantern-gold accents.
- **Clothing silhouette:** light layered robes, fitted traveling clothes,
  supple wraps, and shaped armor that frame the face and accommodate hair and
  fur. The modeling clothes in the retained female image are not heritage
  uniform or a mandatory cosmetic.

Vesh heritage names remain soft and echoing. Nhalen and Saeril favor breathy
openings, liquid consonants, and paired vowels. Chosen titles remember an act,
journey, or guiding encounter rather than claiming spiritual rank over others.

Their movement is composed and attentive, with deliberate head turns, flowing
transitions, and restrained hair and fur follow-through. Voice and movement
identity retain the shared attack contact, recovery, and telegraph rules.

### Selaen, Lanternrest, and the Quieting

Selaen remains an airborne convoy suspended and steered by resonant lantern
engines riding observed Ember currents. Learned observation and fallible spirit
guidance inform routes; no encounter guarantees safe passage. Routine travel
consumes neither captive spirits nor captured Star-light, and Vesh bodies
cannot fly. Its caravans carry supplies, witnesses, and records between places.

Mother-Lanterns are constructed semi-living meeting vessels where willing
spirits and living listeners gather. They permit refusal and departure; they
do not carry Selaen or define Vesh anatomy.

The Duskbreak disrupted the places and histories to which many spirits belong.
Vesh keepers now face the same dimming as every people, with the added duty of
hearing presences that communities may no longer recognize. The Ashen Choir
exploits the desire to preserve them by promising a final harmony.

In the Quieting, distinct spirits begin repeating one account as the Choir
forces their voices toward the Onefold Vault. Lanternrest investigates the
lost differences, Selaen must choose which endangered community it can reach,
and Nhalen and Saeril confront the cost of treating guidance as certainty.
The [world storyline](world-and-settlements.md#the-quieting---working-name) and
[character arcs](characters-and-factions.md) define the connected narrative.

## Character Presentation Contracts

### Appearance Data

Appearance uses common identity fields plus a race-discriminated feature set.
The server validates each saved combination.

Common appearance categories are:

- race;
- sex: male or female;
- curated body preset;
- constrained visual-height preset;
- age-presentation preset;
- face preset;
- skin tone (surface tone for Kell; coat tone for Vesh);
- markings; and
- voice.

Race-specific categories are:

- **Aurin:** hair style, hair color, and hair-dye treatment.
- **Kell:** horn form, plate pattern, mineral tones, mineral finish, wear,
  engraving, and inlay.
- **Vesh:** hair style, coat texture, white-fur pattern, and face-surface finish.
  The selected coat is obsidian-black with white markings and golden eyes;
  alternate palette choices remain TBD.

Race-specific fields must not become nullable gameplay inputs scattered across
class, faction, ability, equipment, or progression data.

Portable cultural pigments, symbols, and adornment styles belong to shared
marking, cosmetic, or equipment layers. They are not locked inside one
people's race-feature block.

### Combat Envelope and Rig

All people and sexes share:

- one authoritative combat capsule;
- equivalent visible height and limb-reach bounds;
- one shared combat-presentation skeleton and weapon-socket alignment contract;
- one shared camera and aim-presentation anchor contract that never selects a
  target or originates an authoritative trace;
- identical attack traces, authored contact frames, root-motion distances, and
  recovery timing; and
- the same readable telegraph requirements.

Kell horn, ear, and plate attachments and any auxiliary visual bones, together
with Vesh hair and fur presentation bones, serve presentation only. They may
drive secondary animation and visual-only attachment sockets, but
never collision, authoritative traces, the shared weapon-presentation sockets,
reach, root motion, authored contact timing, or gameplay state.

Visible joint scaling remains inside the validated rig bounds. Animation
warping and IK keep visible hands, weapons, feet, and attack contact aligned
with the shared presentation sockets, root motion, ground plane, and authored
contact frames for every approved body and height preset. Sockets visualize
that server-owned timeline; they never establish a hit volume, window, contact,
or selected target.

Exact capsule dimensions, visual-height limits, and skeleton measurements
remain TBD until the combat prototype validates the reference body.

### Equipment Presentation

One equipment item has one gameplay identity, stat definition, and eligibility
rule. Its visible fitting set uses race-and-sex-specific bases plus the morphs,
feature accommodations, or additional visual variants needed for every
approved body, height, horn, plate, hair, fur, and natural-face combination.

Each people defines stable visual attachment roots and protected fit envelopes
for required anatomy. Exact measurements remain TBD with the reference rigs,
but equipment may not intrude into those envelopes or move them to imply a
different combat surface.

- Helmets accommodate Aurin hair, Kell horns and external ears, and the Vesh
  natural face and hair without removing biological anatomy.
- Shoulder, chest, and collar pieces fit Kell plate contours and neck movement.
- Gauntlets and boots accommodate Kell claws, thumb articulation, and
  three-toed feet while preserving shared grips and foot placement.
- Vesh gauntlets fit two fingers and a thumb, and boots fit their solid tapered
  feet, while preserving shared grip sockets and ground contact.
- Vesh collars, cloaks, and back equipment accommodate coat and hair volume;
  no back-veil accommodation is required by the revised anatomy.
- Biological structures never retract, disappear, or become armor to solve
  equipment fitting.
- Cultural appearances are wearable by every people through fitted variants.
- A visible equipment set is not ready for supported character creation until
  its extreme supported preset combinations pass clipping and animation review.

### Animation Presentation

Each people receives a full visual movement identity through shared gameplay
sources plus race-specific base poses, transitions, secondary motion, and
additive layers.

Animation variation must not change alignment with authored contact frames,
weapon-presentation sockets, authoritative traces, camera or aim-presentation
anchors, root-motion distances, recovery timing, or readable telegraphs. Male
and female presentation may differ within the same rule.

### Visibility and Accessibility

Vesh eye light, Kell crystal and eye light, and Kell mineral reflectivity are
presentation-normalized. Exposure, distance LOD, and common combat-readability
rules must prevent race from
creating a visibility, targeting, tracking, or concealment advantage.

Vesh golden eye light uses one authored luminance envelope across expression
and LODs. Coat color, white pattern, and fur detail remain readable under common
exposure rules; they cannot create a concealment or tracking advantage. Player
choices cannot disable the eye light or exceed its envelope. Kell crystal and
eye light likewise use a bounded authored luminance
envelope; appearance choices cannot turn biological glow off or make it a
spotlight. Exact values remain TBD. Accessibility and graphics settings may
change presentation only if
the result preserves equivalent identification and tracking opportunity across
all peoples.

Color alone must not communicate required emotion, faction, hostility, or
combat state.

## Art Validation Gate

A playable-people concept is not approved for production until review includes:

- front, side, and back adult turnarounds for male and female Aurin, Kell, and
  Vesh;
- shared-capsule, limb-reach, skeleton, and weapon-presentation-socket
  overlays;
- the broad body-preset range inside the constrained height envelope;
- extreme supported body, height, age, horn, plate, hair, fur, and face
  combinations;
- grayscale silhouettes at representative gameplay-camera distance;
- all supported class weapon grips and representative contact poses;
- camera, aim-origin, hand, weapon, foot-plant, and authored-contact alignment;
- fitted helmet, shoulder, chest, cloak, and back-equipment stress cases;
- horn, ear, plate, claw, fur, natural-face, hair, and cloth clipping checks in
  locomotion and combat;
- facial expression, eye behavior, voice presentation, and facial-rig checks
  across every face preset; use mouth visemes where the anatomy supports them,
  and eye, head, and voice expression for Vesh without opening a human mouth;
- low-light and high-exposure checks for Vesh eye light, fur contrast, and Kell
  crystal surfaces, inner radiance, and glowing eyes;
- LOD checks that preserve identity without changing combat readability; and
- examples showing each people outside its heritage clothing and in both
  faction presentations once final faction heraldry exists.

Reject any concept that implies different hitboxes or reach, hides mandatory
anatomy to fit equipment, maps race to faction, or revives a prohibited stock
fantasy or animal-head silhouette.

## Decisions Still Open

Do not invent:

- exact lifespan years;
- final Kell hand digit topology, grip deformation, and alternate mineral or
  glow-color options;
- Vesh physiology beyond the established mortal embodied form, exact face,
  hair, coat and marking option ranges, grip deformation, and alternate coat
  or eye-color choices;
- exact body, capsule, skeleton, or visual-height measurements;
- exact protected fit-envelope, joint-scaling, IK, and luminance measurements;
- the number of presets or options for any common or race-specific appearance
  category;
- final faction names, colors, heraldry, or race-specific faction styling;
- final language grammar or complete naming lexicons; or
- production budgets and release quantities for fitted equipment and animation
  variants.

These decisions remain TBD until their owning design or prototype evidence
resolves them.
