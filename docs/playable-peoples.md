# Playable Peoples of Aetheln

## Document Status

This is the canonical anatomy, visual identity, cultural-heritage, character
creation, animation, and equipment-presentation reference for the playable
peoples of Aetheln.

The Game Design Bible governs high-level product behavior. This document
governs playable-people detail. Characters, settlements, classes, factions,
progression, and technical architecture remain governed by their specialized
canonical documents.

## Shared Canon

The three confirmed playable peoples are Aurin, Kell, and Vesh.

- The Waking Star dreamed each people separately into existence.
- All three peoples predate the Duskbreak. No confirmed account identifies
  which people was dreamed first.
- Aurin, Kell, and Vesh are distinct biologies and do not produce biological
  hybrids with one another. Cross-people families form through partnership,
  adoption, and chosen kinship.
- Each people maintains heritage languages alongside a shared common language.
- Every playable people supports male and female adult characters across a
  broad visible age range.
- Every people and sex may use every supported class and join either player
  faction.
- Race, sex, body, age, face, voice, and other appearance choices never change
  attributes, combat power, collision, reach, traces, timing, readable
  telegraphs, loot probability, or progression.
- All people use a narrow, overlapping visual-height range and broad curated
  body presets, including lean, soft, muscular, and heavy-set directions.
- Male and female presentations must read as clearly distinct within each
  people while retaining broad overlap in height, build, age presentation, and
  voice. Sex-linked face, body, and secondary-anatomy anchors are defined below
  and remain inside the shared combat envelope.

Visual designs must not reduce the peoples to renamed stock fantasy races,
animal heads on humanoid bodies, or extreme playable size classes.

## Culture, Faction, and Individual Identity

Each people has recognizable heritage materials, motifs, naming traditions,
and movement language. Those roots do not prescribe personality, profession,
class, homeland, or faction.

Each people's heritage identity carries a stable semantic ID with a working
display name (Issue #104): Aurin use `heritage.menders_brace` (Mender's Brace),
Kell use `heritage.memory_nail` (Memory Nail), and Vesh use
`heritage.foreseen_path` (Foreseen Path). IDs remain stable when display names
change. These are cultural origins and working names for learnable Heritage
traditions, not innate racial traits. Any character of any people or sex can
learn all three under the same eligibility and gameplay rules; originating from
a culture grants no automatic unlock, stronger version, or altered cost.
Learning and equipping a tradition is separate from race and appearance.
Neither ancestry nor presentation changes combat statistics, hitboxes, reach,
timing, loot, or the authoritative behavior of a learned Heritage. The
acquisition and loadout contract is defined in
[Progression, Loot, and Skein Weaving](progression-loot-and-skein.md).

- Aurin are not biologically aligned with the Crowned Ledger (the provisional
  faction display name superseding Dawn Concordat under Issue #104).
- Kell are not biologically aligned with the Hundred Witnesses (superseding
  Unbound Flame under Issue #104).
- Vesh are not biologically neutral.
- Any people may live within, inherit, learn, or wear another people's culture.
- Cultural equipment and cosmetics must have fitted visual variants for every
  supported people and sex.
- Faction identity must come from explicit faction heraldry, clothing,
  objectives, territory, Doctrine, and story presentation rather than anatomy.

## Aurin

### Identity and Anatomy

Aurin are the human people of Aetheln. "Human" is an out-of-world explanatory
term; people in Aetheln call them Aurin.

Aurin use fully human anatomy and the full natural range of human skin tones,
hair colors, hair textures, faces, builds, and visible aging. They have no
innate glow, glass, Ember mark, mutation, or supernatural organ. Individual
magic exposure, injury, scars, cosmetics, and Duskbreak effects remain
individual conditions rather than Aurin biology.

Male and female Aurin use clearly distinct base human face, torso-form,
fat-distribution, and musculature directions across overlapping heights and
limb lengths. Every sex still supports the full curated body-preset,
age-presentation, and voice ranges.

### Visual Language

Aurin should feel resilient and burdened rather than pristine or generically
heroic.

- **Materials:** maintained steel, dark wood, heavy woven cloth, visible
  mending, and inherited pieces.
- **Motifs:** repaired lines, joined breaks, reinforced seams, layered borders,
  and tally marks used for family records, vows, debts, or survival counts.
- **Heritage palette:** charcoal, iron, rust-red Ember warmth, and restrained
  gold light.
- **Clothing silhouette:** layered workwear with structured shoulders, wrapped
  waists, overlapping panels, and visible repairs.
- **Markings:** the full range of human scars, tattoos, paint, cosmetic
  pigments, ritual marks, and natural features.

These are heritage defaults, not compulsory clothing or a faction uniform.

### Culture, Names, and Motion

Aurin cultures argue over whether survival means repairing inherited systems or
dismantling them to build something genuinely new.

Aurin heritage names are grounded and familiar. Flowing given names such as
Kaelen, Serra, and Aldrec may pair with terse lineage, place, or trade surnames
such as Dross, Vale, and Fane.

Aurin movement is grounded and purposeful. Weight settles clearly, gestures
conserve effort, and actions appear practiced. This movement identity is
presentation only and does not change gameplay timing.

Aurin voices cover a broad human range. Accent and vocal character may express
region and culture independently from faction or class.

## Kell

### Identity and Anatomy

Kell are clearly nonhuman organic-mineral people. Living skin covers most of
the body while an integrated mineral frame emerges at the skull, selected
facial planes, upper torso, shoulders, and joints.

Required Kell anatomy includes:

- a readable humanoid face built from distinct faceted planes;
- articulated faceted-lens eyes rather than human eyes;
- a low faceted nasal bridge ending in narrow breathing apertures;
- an expressive mouth;
- recessed mineral-lined auditory openings instead of external ears;
- five-digit hands with shared weapon-grip geometry and mineral joint caps;
- mineral crown ridges integrated into the skull; and
- a segmented mineral mantle supporting the upper spine and shoulders.

Kell do not grow hair. Crown shape, mineral family, finish, wear, engraving,
and inlay fill the biological head-customization role. Fitted crown and mantle
adornments use the shared cosmetic or equipment layer.

The crown is sensitive to physical resonance, and the mantle braces the Kell
upper body. These biological functions grant no detection, protection, armor,
or other gameplay benefit. Crown, mantle, and joint structures are never
included in authoritative targeting beyond the shared combat collision.

Kell skin spans living clay, ash, ochre, and slate tones. Their exposed mineral
frames support broad non-glowing natural families such as quartz, smoky glass,
jade, amber, ironstone, and amethyst. Innate Kell glow is not part of the
baseline design.

### Sex, Age, and Variation

Male Kell use outward buttressed crown lines and tiered mantle geometry.
Female Kell use inward nested crown lines and overlapping mantle strata.
These sex-linked crown and mantle anchor families do not cross, while
individual presets provide substantial variation within each family. Both use
the same visual scale, limb reach, combat envelope, and broad body-preset,
age-presentation, and voice ranges.

Kell face presets may vary facet arrangement, brow and jaw planes, lens shape,
mouth shape, and breathing-aperture placement while retaining every required
Kell facial anchor.

Kell are very long-lived and may span several Aurin generations, but they are
not immortal. Exact lifespan years remain TBD. Age appears through added
mineral strata, inclusions, softened polish, fine chips, repair, and slow
organic aging rather than ever-increasing body size.

Crown and mantle growth follows a balanced underlying structure with natural
asymmetry from age, wear, damage, and repair. Kell may voluntarily engrave and
inlay exposed mineral anatomy. These modifications are cosmetic records and
art, never sources of power.

### Visual Language

Kell should inspire awe and unease while remaining emotionally expressive.
Their beauty is architectural and strange rather than conventionally human.

- **Materials:** fine etched dark metal, suspended memory glass,
  tension-woven cloth, and precision inlay.
- **Motifs:** nested facets and geological strata representing memories held
  within memories.
- **Heritage palette:** graphite, smoked black, deep umber, muted metal, and
  selective natural-mineral accents.
- **Clothing silhouette:** angular cloth planes suspended from precision
  fittings around the biological mantle.

Kell craft emphasizes precision and remembrance rather than stock stonefolk
bulk or natural armor.

### Culture, Names, and Motion

Kell cultures argue over whether every memory must be preserved or whether
forgetting, privacy, and release are necessary for life.

Memory preservation is learned craft using tools and study. It is not a
universal Kell ability, and other peoples may learn its techniques.

Kell heritage names are resonant and weighty. Names such as Corvath and Vothram
favor deep consonants and held internal rhythms without requiring one repeated
suffix.

Kell movement appears slow and monumental through posture, easing, weight
transfer, and mantle follow-through. Startup, contact, recovery, root motion,
and travel timing remain identical to the shared gameplay source.

Kell voices support broad pitch and character choices with subtle mineral
resonance rather than one fixed vocal archetype.

### Corvath's Exception

Corvath is a unique living-glass Kell created by the Duskbreak. His silhouette
remains Kell, but his complete material state is not ordinary Kell biology, a
heritable subtype, or an available player appearance.

Vothram forged the instruments used to cause the Duskbreak, confirming that
Kell existed before the catastrophe. Graefell's first Kell awakening therefore
predates the canyon's later glassing.

## Vesh

### Identity and Anatomy

Vesh are clearly nonhuman dream-veiled people. They have no aquatic ancestry,
anatomy, adaptations, or animal-derived head structure.

Required Vesh anatomy includes:

- matte organic skin in smoke, mauve, muted umber, dusk-violet, and charcoal
  families;
- slow-moving subdermal light with one stable, player-selected base hue;
- a smooth face showing only two large eyes at rest;
- deep reflective eyes with drifting inner light and no visible white or fixed
  iris;
- a fine mouth seam that opens for speech and eating and closes completely
  flush;
- paired subtle breathing vents beneath the jaw and along the upper neck;
- conventional hair across the full familiar texture range, with natural
  colors and restrained dream hues;
- five long digits with shared weapon-grip geometry and moving light pathways;
  and
- two soft, non-grasping living back-veils.

The paired back-veils are permanent biological organs. They fold close to the
body for clothing, move involuntarily with emotion, and regulate sleep and
dream intensity. They do not attack, grasp, detect enemies, enable flight,
predict the future, change collision, or grant gameplay utility.

At rest, Vesh emotion remains readable through eyes, posture, voice,
inner-light movement, and veil response. Inner-light emotion changes
brightness, speed, and topology rather than the character's selected hue.

Vesh face presets may vary outer face contour, eye size and angle, mouth-seam
shape, and vent pattern while retaining two visible eyes at rest and the
required flush mouth seam.

### Sex, Age, and Variation

Male Vesh use long forked veil contours and straighter inner-light paths.
Female Vesh use broad scalloped veil contours and looping inner-light paths.
These sex-linked veil and light-path anchor families do not cross, while
individual presets provide substantial variation within each family. Both use
equal visual scale, shared limb reach, the same combat envelope, and broad
body-preset, age-presentation, and voice ranges.

Paired veils echo rather than perfectly mirror one another. They share one
underlying form with subtle differences in contour and light timing.

Vesh live moderately longer than Aurin without approaching Kell longevity.
Exact years remain TBD. Age appears through veil scars and contour changes,
calmer inner-light movement, and subtle changes to hair and skin.

Vesh may decorate their back-veils with soft fittings, edge bindings, safe
dyes, and lightweight cloth attachments. Cutting and piercing are not part of
the default playable appearance system.

### Visual Language

Vesh should inspire wonder and mystery. Character creation may range from
beautiful to unsettling without reducing their anatomy to an animal hybrid or
obscuring readable expression.

- **Materials:** layered veilcloth, lacquered wood, translucent resin, and
  responsive luminous thread.
- **Motifs:** slightly offset echoed contours and repeated lines that suggest
  remembered alternatives.
- **Heritage palette:** indigo, plum, and lantern gold.
- **Clothing silhouette:** open-backed layered garments with soft collars,
  split backs, floating cloth layers, and luminous stitched contours that
  protect and frame the biological veils.

### Culture, Names, and Motion

Vesh cultures argue over foreknowledge and free choice. Some trust trained
dream-readers to guide action; others believe predictions manufacture the
futures they claim to prevent.

Vivid dreaming is common Vesh experience, but prophetic dream-reading is a
learned discipline using dream-tech. It is not an innate racial power, and
other peoples may study it.

Vesh heritage names are soft and echoing. Names such as Nhalen and Saeril favor
breathy openings, liquid consonants, and paired vowel sounds.

Vesh movement flows with delayed visual echoes. Hair, clothing, inner light,
and back-veils complete slightly offset follow-through while gameplay contact
and recovery remain shared.

Vesh voices support broad pitch and character choices with subtle layered
harmonics rather than one fixed sound.

### Selaen and the Sky

Selaen is an airborne convoy of lantern-craft and dream-tech platforms, not a
waterborne settlement. Resonant lantern engines read and ride predicted Ember
currents to suspend and steer the convoy. Vesh bodies cannot fly, and routine
Selaen travel does not consume captured Star-light as fuel.

Mother-Lanterns remain semi-living cultural and dream-reading structures. They
do not carry Selaen or define Vesh biology.

## Character Presentation Contracts

### Appearance Data

Appearance uses common identity fields plus a race-discriminated feature set.
The server validates each saved combination.

Common appearance categories are:

- race;
- sex: male or female;
- curated body preset;
- constrained visual-height preset;
- age-presentation preset;
- face preset;
- skin tone;
- markings; and
- voice.

Race-specific categories are:

- **Aurin:** hair style, hair color, and hair-dye treatment.
- **Kell:** crown, mantle, mineral family, mineral finish, wear, engraving, and
  inlay.
- **Vesh:** hair, veil form, veil adornment, inner-light hue, and light pattern.

Race-specific fields must not become nullable gameplay inputs scattered across
class, faction, ability, equipment, or progression data.

Portable cultural pigments, symbols, and adornment styles belong to shared
marking, cosmetic, or equipment layers. They are not locked inside one
people's race-feature block.

### Combat Envelope and Rig

All people and sexes share:

- one authoritative combat capsule;
- equivalent visible height and limb-reach bounds;
- one shared combat-presentation skeleton and weapon-socket alignment contract;
- one shared camera and aim-presentation anchor contract that never selects a
  target or originates an authoritative trace;
- identical attack traces, authored contact frames, root-motion distances, and
  recovery timing; and
- the same readable telegraph requirements.

Kell crown and mantle bones and Vesh back-veil bones are auxiliary visual bones.
They may drive secondary animation and visual-only attachment sockets, but
never collision, authoritative traces, the shared weapon-presentation sockets,
reach, root motion, authored contact timing, or gameplay state.

Visible joint scaling remains inside the validated rig bounds. Animation
warping and IK keep visible hands, weapons, feet, and attack contact aligned
with the shared presentation sockets, root motion, ground plane, and authored
contact frames for every approved body and height preset. Sockets visualize
that server-owned timeline; they never establish a hit volume, window, contact,
or selected target.

Exact capsule dimensions, visual-height limits, and skeleton measurements
remain TBD until the combat prototype validates the reference body.

### Equipment Presentation

One equipment item has one gameplay identity, stat definition, and eligibility
rule. Its visible fitting set uses race-and-sex-specific bases plus the morphs,
feature accommodations, or additional visual variants needed for every
approved body, height, crown, mantle, hair, and veil combination.

Each people defines stable visual attachment roots and protected fit envelopes
for required anatomy. Exact measurements remain TBD with the reference rigs,
but equipment may not intrude into those envelopes or move them to imply a
different combat surface.

- Helmets accommodate Aurin hair, Kell crowns, and Vesh hair without removing
  biological anatomy.
- Shoulder and chest pieces fit around Kell mantles.
- Cloaks and back equipment split, channel, or mount around Vesh back-veils.
- Biological structures never retract, disappear, or become armor to solve
  equipment fitting.
- Cultural appearances are wearable by every people through fitted variants.
- A visible equipment set is not ready for supported character creation until
  its extreme supported preset combinations pass clipping and animation review.

### Animation Presentation

Each people receives a full visual movement identity through shared gameplay
sources plus race-specific base poses, transitions, secondary motion, and
additive layers.

Animation variation must not change alignment with authored contact frames,
weapon-presentation sockets, authoritative traces, camera or aim-presentation
anchors, root-motion distances, recovery timing, or readable telegraphs. Male
and female presentation may differ within the same rule.

### Visibility and Accessibility

Vesh light and Kell mineral reflectivity are presentation-normalized. Exposure,
distance LOD, and common combat-readability rules must prevent race from
creating a visibility, targeting, tracking, or concealment advantage.

All Vesh hues, patterns, emotional states, and LODs use one authored luminance
envelope. Player choices cannot disable the biological light or exceed that
envelope. Accessibility and graphics settings may change presentation only if
the result preserves equivalent identification and tracking opportunity across
all peoples.

Color alone must not communicate required emotion, faction, hostility, or
combat state.

## Art Validation Gate

A playable-people concept is not approved for production until review includes:

- front, side, and back adult turnarounds for male and female Aurin, Kell, and
  Vesh;
- shared-capsule, limb-reach, skeleton, and weapon-presentation-socket
  overlays;
- the broad body-preset range inside the constrained height envelope;
- extreme supported body, height, age, crown, mantle, hair, and veil
  combinations;
- grayscale silhouettes at representative gameplay-camera distance;
- all supported class weapon grips and representative contact poses;
- camera, aim-origin, hand, weapon, foot-plant, and authored-contact alignment;
- fitted helmet, shoulder, chest, cloak, and back-equipment stress cases;
- crown, mantle, veil, hair, and cloth clipping checks in locomotion and combat;
- facial expression, eye behavior, speech-viseme, and facial-rig checks across
  every face preset;
- low-light and high-exposure checks for Vesh light and Kell minerals;
- LOD checks that preserve identity without changing combat readability; and
- examples showing each people outside its heritage clothing and in both
  faction presentations once final faction heraldry exists.

Reject any concept that implies different hitboxes or reach, hides mandatory
anatomy to fit equipment, maps race to faction, or revives a prohibited stock
fantasy or animal-head silhouette.

## Decisions Still Open

Do not invent:

- exact lifespan years;
- exact body, capsule, skeleton, or visual-height measurements;
- exact protected fit-envelope, joint-scaling, IK, and luminance measurements;
- the number of presets or options for any common or race-specific appearance
  category;
- final faction names, colors, heraldry, or race-specific faction styling;
- final language grammar or complete naming lexicons; or
- production budgets and release quantities for fitted equipment and animation
  variants.

These decisions remain TBD until their owning design or prototype evidence
resolves them.
